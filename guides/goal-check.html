Goal Check Report

How to find a student and assign demerits in the Goal Check Report.

Overview

Use the Goal Check Report to review student Goal Checks and record demerits when necessary.

1. Open the Goal Check Report

  1. Sign in to STMS using your staff account.
  2. Open the Goal Check Report section.
  3. Select the Students tab.

2. Assign a demerit

  1. Find the relevant student in the list.
  2. Open the student's Goal Check record.
  3. Choose the appropriate demerit option and save the change.

Note: Confirm the student and the selected reason before saving. The exact controls may differ depending on your STMS permissions and current interface version.

Troubleshooting

  • If the Students tab is unavailable, check that your account has the required permissions.
  • If a student is missing, verify the selected class, date, or reporting period.
  • If the page behaves unexpectedly, record the steps and contact the STMS administrator.
